# Never guess a legal name

Don't infer someone's legal or full name from a username, handle, email
local-part, or commit author string. A username is not a name, and a wrong guess
is harder to notice than an omission — it reads as confident and correct until
the person it names says otherwise.

Ask directly, or leave the name out of the artefact until it's confirmed.
Applies anywhere a name gets written down for someone else to read: changelog
entries, commit messages, generated documentation, PR descriptions, and the
copyright holder name when writing or filling in a `LICENSE` file.
